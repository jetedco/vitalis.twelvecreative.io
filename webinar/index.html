    The Session

    What You’ll Learn

    One focused hour on the decision itself — not a sales pitch dressed up as a class.

    1. 1

      Own vs. lease, in real numbers. How practice owners actually compare a mortgage on their own office against decades of rent — and where each option wins.

    2. 2

      What a medical building must get right. Ceiling heights, corridor widths, patient drop-off, parking, telemedicine infrastructure — the features that decide whether a space works for care.

    3. 3

      Why location compounds. What being inside an established medical ecosystem does for referrals, recruiting, and patient convenience.

    4. 4

      The investor’s lens. What to investigate in medical office assets — tenant quality, lease structures, and the questions to ask before committing.

    5. 5

      Where Vitalis Tower fits. The project, the district, the delivery timeline — presented plainly, so you can judge the fit yourself.

    6. 6

      Your next step, if you want one. What a private presentation covers and how to arrange a conversation with the sales team.

    Built for Two Kinds of Buyers

    Owner-users and investors weigh this decision differently. The session speaks to both — separately.

    Owner–Users

    Your practice, in your building.

    Physicians, dentists, wellness and professional practices weighing a move or expansion — and tired of renewing someone else’s lease. You’ll leave knowing how to run the own-vs-lease comparison for your own numbers.

    Investors

    An asset with tenants who stay.

    Investors evaluating medical and professional office ownership — a category built on solid tenants, long-term leases, and NNN structures. You’ll leave with the due-diligence questions that matter.

    Exploring Both

    Occupy part. Hold the rest.

    Professionals considering a space for their own use today and an income asset over time. The session shows how both lenses apply to the same building.

    Before You Ask

    Is this free? What’s the catch?

    It’s free, and the format is education first: the ownership decision, the building, and the district. At the end there’s one optional next step — booking a private presentation with the sales team. No purchase discussions happen on the webinar itself.

    Will pricing and availability be covered?

    Current pricing and unit availability change as the project sells, so they’re handled in private presentations where the team can show you exactly what’s open. The webinar covers everything you need to decide whether that conversation is worth your time.

    I’m a physician evaluating space for my own practice. Is this for me?

    Yes — owner-users are the session’s primary audience. The own-vs-lease comparison, the clinical building features, and the district walkthrough are built around a practice owner’s decision.

    I’m an investor, not a practitioner. Will I get anything out of it?

    A dedicated segment covers the investor’s lens: what makes medical office tenants distinctive, how lease structures in this category work, and the due-diligence questions to bring to any project — including this one.

    What time is the webinar in my timezone?

    Sessions run on Eastern Time (Miami’s timezone). The registration box above shows the next date, and your confirmation page and calendar invitation translate it into your local time automatically.

    What if I register and can’t make it?

    You’ll receive a replay link after the session, valid for a limited window — and you can register again for a future date at any time.

    How do I join on the day?

    Your confirmation email (and text, if you opt in) contains your personal access link. It opens the session room, which unlocks shortly before start time.
