The hour, in six parts

What we’ll cover

  1. Own versus lease, in plain numbers. How firms compare a mortgage on their own floor against decades of rent — and where each choice genuinely wins.
  2. What to look for in a building. Ceiling heights, conference facilities, arrival experience, parking — the quiet details that decide how an office serves a business.
  3. The address itself. What an established Aventura location does for clients, recruiting, and the working day.
  4. The investor’s view. Tenant quality, lease length, and triple-net structures — the questions worth asking of any office asset.
  5. Where Vitalis Tower fits. The project, the spaces, and the delivery timeline, presented plainly.
  6. A next step, if you want one. How a private presentation works, and how to arrange it.

Two ways in

Who it’s for

Owner–occupiers

Law, finance, consulting, design, wellness — established firms and professional practices that would rather build equity in their own address than renew someone else’s lease. Spaces run from 500 to 20,000 square feet.

Investors

Buyers considering professional office as an asset class — a category known for solid tenants, long leases, and triple-net terms. You’ll leave with the due-diligence questions that matter.
