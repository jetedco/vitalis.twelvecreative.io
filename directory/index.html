Front of House

The Six Public Pages

In the order a buyer meets them. Every date on every page comes from one schedule in the central config.

1 /webinar/

Registration

The front door. Shows the next open session (auto-rolls at the 15-minute cutoff), collects name, email, phone, SMS consent, and the interest selector — practice / investment / both — that personalizes everything downstream. Submits to the Vitalis GoHighLevel intake and only reports success once the CRM acknowledges it.

countdown + cutoff rollover already-registered recovery honest failure + retry

2 /webinar/confirmed/

Confirmation

Locks the date in. Keeps the registrant pinned to their session (via ?session=) even after the public page rolls to next week. Five one-tap calendar actions — Google, Apple, Outlook.com, Microsoft 365, universal .ics — all built from the same instant, plus a timed early-booking unlock and the live join link on session day.

5 calendar doors countdown → join flip timed booking unlock

3 /webinar/live/

Session Room

Where the webinar plays. The broadcast is locked to the wall clock — everyone, including late joiners, sees the same moment, no scrubbing. Countdown before, ended slate after, and the private-presentation booking reveals itself mid-session. Side chat is moderated: messages go only to the team. The attendee counter is real presence or nothing — never simulated.

pre → live → post phases mid-session CTA unlock moderated chat
Open page →
4 /webinar/replay/

Replay

The safety net for no-shows and rewatchers. Resolves one of six states from the config — upcoming, live-now, replay pending, available, expired, or no registration found — and says so plainly. Watch time is measured by seconds actually played, so skipping to the end never counts as “watched.”

7-day window (config) honest watch tracking next-step CTAs

5 /consultation/

Book a Private Presentation

The commercial next step. Embeds the Vitalis sales calendar, carries known contact details and campaign attribution into the booking, and reorders its pitch for owner-users vs investors. A selected-but-unfinished slot becomes the CRM’s abandoned-booking trigger; a confirmed booking is the funnel’s primary conversion.

interest-adapted copy prefill + attribution primary conversion

6 /consultation/confirmed/

Appointment Confirmed

Closes the loop: what happens next, how to reschedule, what to bring (numbers for owner-users, underwriting questions for investors), and the Sales Center address for in-person meetings. The appointment is a separate calendar event from the webinar — from here, the sales team and CRM follow-up take over.

Engine Room

Behind the Scenes

Nothing here is visitor-facing — but every page above runs on it.

Central Config

/config/funnel.js (+ PHP twin) — the single source of truth: schedule & timezone, event title, cutoff, cancellations, access mode, booking calendar, CRM keys, analytics, replay window. Change it once; every page follows.

Session Clock

/js/core.js — the shared engine. Computes the next open session in America/New_York with DST handled correctly, builds all five calendar links from one instant, and powers countdowns, reveals, and the motion system. 24 unit tests in /tests/.

Attribution

/js/attribution.js — remembers the first ad touch (UTMs, click IDs) for 90 days and hands it to the CRM at registration and to the booking calendar at scheduling. Campaign data only; never personal info in analytics.

Calendar File Server

/webinar/confirmed/event.php — serves the real .ics invite (the reliable iPhone path) with a stable event ID, so re-downloading never duplicates the event. On static hosting the pages quietly switch to a browser-built identical file.

Chat & Presence

chat.php + presence.php — attendee messages (send-only, with phone numbers for follow-up) and a real headcount heartbeat. Requires the PHP host; on static hosting the room says chat isn’t connected rather than pretending.

CRM Blueprint

/crm/README.md — the GoHighLevel build spec: custom fields, tags, pipeline, and 8 workflows (reminders, attendance marking, no-show, abandoned booking, nurture, reschedule runbook) with every email and SMS written out in /crm/templates/.
